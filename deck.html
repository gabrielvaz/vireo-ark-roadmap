Customisable, but not at any cost

Preference reorders the queue and never empties the critical band

Preference can

Change order and emphasis

  • Promote exam types and sending sites
  • Set the default sort of My queue
  • Flag exams into a personal view
  • Collapse the critical band, count stays visible
Preference cannot

Remove a serious case

  • Drop an exam that crossed a criterion
  • Hide a critical exam behind a filter
  • Switch the Needs attention band off
  • Lower priority because the AI called it normal

Scope of pillar 02

Ten systemic issues behind twenty-two visible ones

  • 1

    No priority or severity token

    Clinical severity has no visual expression. This gates pillar 01.

  • 2

    Line breaks and cramped headers

    Column titles on three lines, next to 500 px of empty margin.

  • 3

    Icon-only actions with no name

    Plus an unlabelled toggle that changes how other controls behave.

  • 4

    No container or width rule

    Four screens crowd their content next to unused space.

  • 5

    Wrong weight on the destructive action

    The button that causes data loss is the loudest in the dialogue.

  • 6

    Localisation falls back to English

    Alongside three competing filter mechanisms on one screen.

The design system exists and is in use. These are gaps in coverage and in discipline, and that distinction decides who fixes what.

PILLAR 04

A seamless journey with MindBeat

Not two products that talk to each other. One journey that happens to span two.

One journey, four steps

Out of VIREO ARK and back, with nothing re-entered

1

The inbox flags it

The Holter arrives carrying its analysis state: processing, ready, or unavailable.

2

The summary is here

Headline findings inside VIREO ARK, enough to triage without opening anything.

3

One step into MindBeat

Same patient, same exam, no second login.

4

Back, already synced

Findings and corrections return to the exam. Conclusion and signature close here.

VIREO ARK does not rebuild the Holter viewer. Beat navigation, artefact judgement and morphology grouping stay in MindBeat.

The mechanism

Synced does not mean merged: three origins stay distinguishable

Today the device interpretation and the human conclusion share the same editable field.

  • During the audit, typing into it overwrote the algorithm’s text, including the infarction finding, with no warning and no history.
  • Syncing MindBeat output into that box would make an existing problem worse.

Honesty about scope

What reaches the stage now, and what needs an API contract

A

Demonstrable now

Analysis state indicator · summary panel from a real analysed exam · the three separated origins · agree and disagree with a trail · the hand-off into MindBeat.

B

Needs backend

True per-exam analysis state · findings travelling both ways · persisted agreement · findings feeding the ranking · chained authentication.

C

Deliberately out

Rebuilding the Holter viewer · example strips per event · importing the derived report into conclusions · an AI performance dashboard.

Column C is not later. These are discard decisions.
