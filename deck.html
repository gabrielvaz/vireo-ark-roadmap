Customisable, but not at any cost

Preference reorders the queue and never empties the critical band

Preference can

Change order and emphasis

  • Promote exam types and sending sites
  • Set the default sort of My queue
  • Flag exams into a personal view
  • Collapse the critical band, count stays visible
Preference cannot

Remove a serious case

  • Drop an exam that crossed a criterion
  • Hide a critical exam behind a filter
  • Switch the Needs attention band off
  • Lower priority because the AI called it normal

Scope of pillar 02

Systemic issues behind what the eye catches:
alignment, visual adjustment, refinement and flow

  • No priority or severity token

    Clinical severity has no visual expression. This gates pillar 01.

  • Alignment and spacing

    Column titles breaking onto three lines next to 500 px of empty margin, and nothing sitting on a shared grid.

  • Visual adjustments across screens

    Icon-only actions with no name, an unlabelled toggle that changes how other controls behave, competing filter mechanisms on one screen.

  • No container or width rule

    Screens crowd their content next to unused space, and the same page reads differently on each monitor.

  • Interface refinement

    The button that causes data loss is the loudest in the dialogue, and localisation falls back to English mid-screen.

  • Transition flow

    States swap without motion and navigation jumps, so nothing tells the eye what changed or where it went.

The proposal

One defined role per persona, applied automatically
and still customisable

01

Applied, not assembled

Four roles ship configured. Creating an account means picking a persona, not walking a permission grid one switch at a time.

02

Customisable where it matters

The engine stays open: a site adapts a role or builds its own. Pre-set is the default, not a ceiling.

03

A focused experience

Each person opens the screen their work starts from, with only the modules that work touches. One product, four coherent experiences.

Who uses VIREO ARK

Four personas, and what each one is there to do

01

Technician

Operates the device and captures the exam in a fixed setting. Does not interpret it. Hospital, primary care, emergency.

Values speed of sending and a clear status for every exam

02

Point-of-care operator

Captures in the field (home care, ambulance, mobile units), often on unstable connectivity and with no local support.

Values delivery that holds up off-site, and proof it arrived

03

Analysing physician

Interprets the exam and signs the report. Overreading centres, private practice, in-hospital reading.

Values reporting speed and AI as assistance, never replacement

04

Department manager

Supervises the operation of a centre, hospital or clinic network. Neither captures nor signs.

Values seeing bottlenecks without opening exams one by one
